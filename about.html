About VantaMitra

వంటమిత్ర – Easy Homemade Recipes

Welcome to VantaMitra, a place where everyday cooking becomes simple, enjoyable and approachable for everyone.

Our goal is to make homemade cooking easier, especially for beginners and anyone looking for practical recipes for everyday meals.

What You'll Find Here

VantaMitra brings together homemade Indian and Andhra recipes in English and Telugu. From breakfast and rice dishes to curries, dals, snacks and chutneys, our recipes are presented with ingredients, step-by-step instructions and helpful cooking tips.

Our Approach

We believe good food does not need to be complicated. Simple ingredients, clear instructions and a little patience can make everyday cooking more enjoyable.

Whether you are learning to cook or looking for ideas for your next meal, VantaMitra is here to make your cooking journey easier.

Our Promise

We aim to share useful, easy-to-follow recipes that celebrate the joy of homemade food and help readers cook with confidence.
